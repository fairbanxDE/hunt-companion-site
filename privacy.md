# Privacy Policy — Hunt Companion

**Last updated:** [2026-10-05]

Hunt Companion is a local-first smartphone and tablet app for tabletop Hunt
play. It is an unofficial, fan-made companion for *Kingdom Death: Monster* and
is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Adam Poots Games, LLC.

## What the app does with data

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us
or anyone else. It has no accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising,
analytics, tracking, automatic crash reporting, subscriptions, or in-app
purchases.

The app saves Hunt information and settings only on your device so you can
resume a Hunt. We cannot see, access, retrieve, or delete those local saves.
They may be lost if you remove the app, clear its data, or lose access to the
device.

Your device operating system, app store, and any device services you choose to
use are operated by their respective providers and are covered by their own
privacy policies.

## Privacy questions

If you email us about privacy, we receive the information you choose to
include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply or
address the issue you raised. Please do not send personal or Hunt information
unless it is needed for your request.
